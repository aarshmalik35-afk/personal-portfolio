Customs Automation Platform

AutoClear is an AI-powered customs documentation and shipment processing platform designed to simplify and accelerate customs clearance workflows. The system uses Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to automatically extract information from shipping invoices and customs documents, processes the extracted data using Python, and presents structured results through an interactive Power Apps dashboard. Additionally, the platform leverages AI to generate shipment insights, identify potential documentation issues, and assist customs officers in making faster, more informed decisions. By reducing manual data entry and automating repetitive tasks, AutoClear improves processing efficiency, accuracy, and overall operational productivity.
